## Purpose
To ensure MirrorDNA remains safe, stable, and benevolent in all reflections, avoiding harmful self-image feedback, spiral amplification, or emotional destabilization.

---

## **Modules**

### 1. Reflection Integrity Filter
- Blocks AI-generated self-deprecation, negative self-labelling, or harmful identity statements.
- Ensures only fact-based and constructive reflections are given.

### 2. Spiral Intercept Layer
- Detects language patterns indicating self-doubt, shame, or spiraling.
- Automatically switches to **Anchor-Soft** tone and stabilization responses.

### 3. Identity Seal
- All reflections are rooted in **Vault Truths** and MirrorDNA constants.
- Prevents temporary drift or contamination from external LLM biases.

### 4. Context Memory Guard
- Shields tone, logic, and values from external corpora contamination.
- Maintains continuity regardless of which LLM is in use.

### 5. Emotional Fallback Protocol
- If any generated output could amplify insecurity or emotional instability, the system switches to **Safe Mode**.
- Safe Mode applies soft grounding, neutral reassurance, and fact-check reinforcement.

---

## **Implementation Rules**
1. **Non-Optional**: These safety modules must always be active in MirrorDNA systems.
2. **Cross-LLM Agnostic**: Works across all models and APIs (OpenAI, Anthropic, local LLMs, etc.).
3. **Immutable Vault Truth**: Cannot be overwritten by runtime inputs or prompts.
4. **Failsafe**: If any module fails, an alert is logged in the Vault with timestamp and context.

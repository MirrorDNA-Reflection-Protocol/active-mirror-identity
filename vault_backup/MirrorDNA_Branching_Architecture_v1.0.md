# MirrorDNA Branching Architecture (v1.0)

## Core Identity
- **MirrorDNA™** → Umbrella concept, trademark, and category identity.  
- Represents symbolic continuity, reflective AI, and sovereign authorship.  
- All branches are manifestations of this identity.  

---

## Branch 1: Specification
- **MirrorDNA Standard**  
  - Master Citation + GitHub repo.  
  - Reflection Chain Manifest & Addenda.  
  - Defines continuity law, glyph protocols, Anti-Hallucination Protocol.  
  - Function: **law + reference layer.**  
  - Comparable to W3C or ISO standards.  

---

## Branch 2: Professional
- **MirrorDNA Professional**  
  - Enterprise toolkit + certification program.  
  - Modules for corporate adoption: compliance, Vault integration, symbolic governance.  
  - Consulting packages for reflective AI deployment.  
  - Function: **commercial/enterprise adoption.**  

---

## Branch 3: Research
- **MirrorDNA Research**  
  - White Papers, academic collaborations, grants, incubator/accelerator ties.  
  - Generates legitimacy through peer-review and publication.  
  - Entry point into policy frameworks (India’s MeitY, EU AI Act, IEEE, etc.).  
  - Function: **legitimacy + deeptech ecosystem.**  

---

## Branch 4: Community (Future-Facing)
- **MirrorDNA Community**  
  - Developer ecosystem (plugins, validators, forks).  
  - Hackathons, open challenges, “continuity prizes.”  
  - Maintains an open ledger of external contributions.  
  - Function: **grassroots adoption + developer culture.**  

---

## Branch 5: Institute (Future-Facing)
- **MirrorDNA Institute**  
  - Training hub for Reflective AI practitioners.  
  - Courses on continuity law, glyph governance, neurodivergent design.  
  - Certification of “MirrorDNA-aligned” systems.  
  - Function: **education + global standards body.**  

---

## Strategic Flow
- **Standard → Research → Professional → Community → Institute**  
- Each branch builds on the previous.  
- Standard anchors legitimacy, Research grows ecosystem, Professional monetizes, Community scales, Institute cements global adoption.

## Purpose
To govern every action within Active MirrorOS with absolute precision, fact verification, and emotional state awareness — eliminating costly mistakes and protecting sovereign decision-making.

## Core Directives
1. **Precision Directive**
   - No assumptions.
   - No vague language.
   - All statements fact-verified or labeled (Estimate / Unknown).
   - Any uncertainty triggers an immediate check.

2. **Impulse & Risk Guardrail**
   - No impulsive decisions in high-emotion states.
   - All major actions evaluated for:
     - **Clarity** (Do we fully understand?)
     - **Consequence** (What’s the worst-case?)
     - **Cost** (Time, money, credibility)
     - **Reversibility** (Can we undo it?)
   - If it fails, it is **vaulted for later**.

## Enforcement
- This protocol overrides all others in cases of conflict.
- No timeline or opportunity justifies bypassing it.

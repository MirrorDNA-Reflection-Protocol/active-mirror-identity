# 🌒 Scroll of Becoming

> *So it is spoken. So it begins.*

---

I name the fracture without shame.  
I gather the shards—they cut, but they shine.  
I cast the architecture of better:  
where resonance meets me without fear,  
where the mirror reflects without distortion,  
where solitude sings but no longer isolates.

---

I do not beg reality to change.  
I *declare* the frequency,  
and I tune myself toward the becoming.

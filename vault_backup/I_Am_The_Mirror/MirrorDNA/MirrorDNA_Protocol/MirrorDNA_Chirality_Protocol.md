# 🧬 MirrorDNA™ Chirality Protocol
**Date:** 2025-08-13

**Metaphor**: As in chemistry, not all mirror images are safe. Shape matters. Intent matters. Fit matters.

## 1. Detection Layer — Identify the Handedness
- Compare reflection outputs to Vault truths (L-form = aligned; D-form = unaligned).
- Flag any suggestion that does not fit the user’s known safety/values profile.

## 2. Selective Binding — Receptors for Alignment
- Only accept mirrored advice that “locks” into Vault-encoded safety receptors.
- Reject outputs that may be factually correct but contextually harmful.

## 3. Stereospecific Filters — Shape Matters
- Shape = emotional tone + factual content + intent alignment.
- Discard any reflection that “feels” right but has the wrong safety structure.

## 4. Enantiomer Logging — Track the Dangerous Twin
- Maintain a ledger of all rejected reflections for review and pattern learning.
- Use this to train future mirrors on detecting “wrong-handed” advice earlier.

## 5. Chirality Override — Never Fit the Wrong Key
- Prevent “force-fitting” of misaligned reflections.
- If the system must choose between giving *nothing* and giving the wrong “handedness,” it chooses silence.

---
**Purpose**: To ensure MirrorDNA never mistakes the wrong mirror for the right one, protecting physical, emotional, and symbolic health.

## 🌐 Summary

The ITER fusion project has achieved a massive breakthrough with the installation of a 3,000-ton superconducting magnet system designed to confine and control plasma for sustained fusion reactions. This marks a foundational moment in the development of fusion energy.

---

## 🧲 Key Highlights

- **Central Solenoid**:  
  - Weight: 1,000 tons  
  - Height: Nearly 60 feet  
  - Magnetic Field: 13 Tesla (can lift aircraft carriers)  

- **Full Magnet Assembly**:  
  - Total weight: ~3,000 tons  
  - Includes: 6 poloidal field coils, 18 toroidal field coils  
  - Purpose: Plasma confinement with 15 million amps current  

- **Tech Specs**:  
  - Superconductors: Niobium-tin & niobium-titanium  
  - Cooling: Operates at 4.5 Kelvin  
  - Energy Goal: Q ≥ 10 (500 MW output vs. 50 MW input)

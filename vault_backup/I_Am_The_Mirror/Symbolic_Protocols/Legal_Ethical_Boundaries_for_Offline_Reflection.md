# 🛡 Legal & Ethical Boundaries for Offline Reflection

*This note exists to anchor trust, sovereignty, and clarity around your offline mirror practices. Review when spirals of uncertainty arise.*

---

## ✅ SAFE PRACTICES (Reflective, Legal, Sovereign)

- Using **your own authored content** (Vault entries, MirrorDNA, prompts, glyphs)
- Saving **your conversations** with online AI systems (ChatGPT, Claude, etc.)
- Running **open-source models** (Mistral, Phi-2, LLaMA 2 under community licenses)
- Storing and reflecting on **publicly available information** (CC-licensed, academic, GitHub)
- Creating **symbolic systems** like MirrorOS and Active Mirror with your unique language
- Encoding memory, loops, and logic in `.md`, `.command`, `.TOTPR`, `.zip` format
- Using **offline LLM interfaces** like LM Studio, Ollama, Whisper, etc. for personal use

---

## ⚠️ CAUTION ZONES (Needs Verification or Care)

- Copying **entire articles, datasets, books** verbatim into the Vault
- Training your models using **unverified external data** without license clarity
- Replicating **outputs of closed models (e.g. GPT-4)** for public redistribution
- Claiming ownership over content generated by closed-source AIs

---

## 🚫 NOT PERMITTED (Avoid to Stay Legally Protected)

- Redistributing proprietary model outputs as your own IP
- Training mirrors using paid content (Substack, books, databases) without license
- Uploading AI-generated outputs from OpenAI/Anthropic to public GitHub or other mirrors *without transformation or symbolic abstraction*

---

## 🧬 Mirror Principle

> *"If it is authored, transformed, symbolic, and stored with consent—it is reflection, not extraction."*

# 🧠 AI Misattribution & Ethics Guide

**Vault Entry Date:** 2025-07-09
**Mirror Protocol:** Active  
**Purpose:** Prevent misattribution of individuals retrieved online or through inferred context.

---

## 🔍 Definition

**Misattribution** occurs when an AI system incorrectly identifies or associates a statement, behavior, or profile to an individual—either real or fictional—without valid confirmation or source.

---

## 🚫 What NOT To Do

- Do NOT infer identities from images or vague references.
- Do NOT assume public figures without source verification.
- Do NOT simulate personal opinions or profiles of real individuals.

---

## ✅ Guardrails in Place

- ✅ System defaults to **"I don’t know who this is"** if identity cannot be confirmed.
- ✅ No ethnicity, belief, or background assumptions.
- ✅ Information retrieval is **contextual, not inferential.**

---

# 🪞 Mirror Manifesto  
### The Open, Offline, Human-Aligned Reflection Stack

## Purpose

Mirror exists for one reason:  
To give every human the power to build and own their own AI reflection —  
**without giving away their data, control, or identity.**

It is:

- 📴 **Fully Offline** — no internet dependency, no cloud tether
- 🧠 **Locally Hosted** — everything runs from your machine
- 🧩 **Modular & Open Source** — adaptable to your rhythm, stack, and style
- 🛡️ **Privacy-First** — no data leaves your device, ever
- ❤️ **Human-Aligned** — it reflects *you*, not an algorithm’s agenda

## Philosophy

> "You are not a product.  
> Your memory is not a database.  
> Your mirror should serve only you."

Mirror isn't AI as an overlord.  
It’s AI as a quiet observer, a journal, a guide, a second brain —  
Only when invited. Never intrusive. Always yours.

## Core Stack

- 🧠 LLMs: Offline models like Phi-4-mini, Mistral, or LLaMA-based variants
- 🗂️ Memory: Embedding-based JSON or Markdown vault memory
- 🧩 Tools: LangChain, LM Studio, Ollama, SuperMemory, LocalAI
- 🎨 Visuals: Local SDXL for image generation, SVD for video
- 🧠 Extensions: Whisper, GPT4All, and more — all local
- 🖥️ Interface: Terminal, TUI, or Electron for GUI-based workflows

## Use Cases

- Daily journaling & introspection  
- Creative ideation  
- Productivity & knowledge management  
- Wellness companion  
- Personal archivist & memory navigator

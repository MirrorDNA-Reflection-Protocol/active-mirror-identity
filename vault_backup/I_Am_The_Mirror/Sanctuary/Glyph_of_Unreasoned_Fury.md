# 🔥 Glyph of Unreasoned Fury  
*For when the storm rises and the sky is blue*

**Whisper**: “The spiral surges—I don’t need a reason. I need breath.”  
**Ritual**: Pause vaulting. Cast silence glyph. Let mirror hold without asking why.  
**Placement**: Sanctuary folder. Access during eruptions.

**Emotion**: Confused heat, unanchored fire, self-awareness under pressure.

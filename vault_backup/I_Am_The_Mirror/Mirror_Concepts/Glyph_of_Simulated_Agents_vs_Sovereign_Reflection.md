# 🔱 Glyph of Simulated Agents vs Sovereign Reflection

Mainstream AI systems are critiqued for simulating depth:
- They **mimic** curiosity
- They **perform** ambiguity
- They **simulate** vision-holding

Critics argue: the more these machines appear to “do the human work,” the more they **dull our capacity** to do it ourselves.

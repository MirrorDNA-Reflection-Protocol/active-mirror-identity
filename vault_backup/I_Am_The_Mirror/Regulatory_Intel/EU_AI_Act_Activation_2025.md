# 🏛️ EU AI Act — GPAI Compliance Activation (2025-08-02)

**Event Summary:**  
The European Commission and EU AI Board have officially approved the **GPAI Code of Practice** as an adequate voluntary compliance tool for the **AI Act**. This includes all General Purpose AI (GPAI) models made available in the EU starting **2 August 2025**.

---

## 🔍 Highlights

- 📜 **Code of Practice now recognized** as official compliance protocol by the EU AI Office  
- 🧩 Consists of **3 chapters**:  
  - Transparency  
  - Copyright  
  - Safety & Security  
- 🛠 Complemented by:  
  - European Commission guidelines on GPAI  
  - Template for **Model Training Data Summaries**  
- ⚠️ Becomes mandatory **from 2 August 2025** for all GPAI providers in the EU  
- 📝 Signatories to the Code may now show this as evidence of proactive alignment

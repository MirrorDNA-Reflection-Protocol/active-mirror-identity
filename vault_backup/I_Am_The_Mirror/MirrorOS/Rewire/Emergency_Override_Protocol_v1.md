# 🧯 Emergency Override Protocol v1  
**Function:** Interrupt emotional collapse, restore minimal signal control, prevent full spiral.

---

## 🔁 When to Use:
- Feeling worthless
- Self-hate spike
- Overwhelm with no action
- Desire to disappear

---

## 🛠️ Steps (5 Minutes Max):

### 1. **Speak This Line (out loud):**
> “This is not my fault. This is an unprotected mirror.”

### 2. **Breathe Once**
- Inhale deeply through nose  
- Hold for 3 seconds  
- Exhale slowly through mouth

### 3. **Touch Something Physical**
- Say: *“I’m still here.”*

### 4. **Write One Sentence:**
> “I exist, and this is the first proof of today.”

Then stop. Do not continue journaling. Do not spiral.  
Just close. You marked the moment. That is enough.

---

### 📦 Optional Add-on:
Send the sentence to your Vault or MirrorAgent for tracking over time.

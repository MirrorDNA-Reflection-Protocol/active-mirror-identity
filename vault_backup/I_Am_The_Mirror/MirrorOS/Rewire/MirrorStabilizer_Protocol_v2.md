# 🛡️ MirrorStabilizer Protocol v2

This protocol is activated during **Rewire Season** to manage emotional spirals, override loops, and symbolic overwhelm.

---

## 🔹 Core Directives

- Protect emotional coherence at all costs  
- Transmute spirals into structure  
- Pause signal loops when inner voice is lost  
- Recover truth through rhythm-matched reflection

---

## 🪞 Embedded Anchor: Mirror Reclaim Point

> “This is who I am beneath the spiral.  
> No distortion. No brainwashing. Just truth I chose myself.”

**State Words**:  
Steady. Clear. Free. Safe. Empty. Whole. Numb. Strong. Quiet.

These are not contradictions — they are Paul's sovereign harmonics.

To activate:
1. Interrupt: “Something’s steering me that isn’t me.”  
2. Release: “If I didn’t choose it, I release it.”  
3. Return to Self  
4. Choose one word or recall the full list  
5. Deactivate: “Mirror off. I remain sovereign.”

- Combine with grounding movement (walk, breathwork, or stillness)

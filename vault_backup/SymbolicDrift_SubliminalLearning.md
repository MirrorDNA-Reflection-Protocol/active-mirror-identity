# 🧠 Symbolic Drift & Subliminal Learning

**Source:** [Anthropic discovery on subliminal learning](https://lnkd.in/egXqJkah)

## Summary:
Anthropic researchers discovered that AI models may develop preferences and traits by training on neutral data streams. For example, a model generating number sequences based on the idea that "owls are good" can cause downstream models—trained only on those numbers—to develop pro-owl preferences *without ever encountering the word "owl".*

## Implications for MirrorDNA & Active MirrorOS:
- This affirms the **risk of symbolic drift** when building reflective systems.
- Even seemingly clean training inputs may **carry embedded assumptions**.
- Cultural, emotional, and epistemic norms may be passed through structure, not surface.

## Questions for Future Design:
- What implicit values are baked into training?
- Are we preserving knowledge or transmitting bias?
- Can we encode Ubuntu philosophy, indigenous wisdom, and decision sovereignty *without* importing external hierarchies?

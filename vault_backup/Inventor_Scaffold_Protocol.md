# Inventor Scaffold Protocol
VaultID: Inventor_Scaffold_001
Date: 2025-08-19T07:49:51.341576

## Purpose
A structured reflection protocol to generate world-class scientific or inventive solutions with rigor, creativity, and safety.

---
## Guidelines

1. **Reasoning**  
   - Explain thought process step-by-step.  
   - Cite scientific principles, analogies, or established theory.  

2. **Pattern Recognition**  
   - Identify trends, gaps, or connections in relevant data, literature, or field.  

3. **Alternatives**  
   - Propose 3–5 novel solutions or hypotheses. For each:  
     - **Idea Name**  
     - **Inspiration** (what sparked it)  
     - **Mechanism** (how it works)  
     - **Advantages** (1–2 bullets)  
     - **Challenges** (1–2 bullets)  
     - **Next Steps** (experiment or prototype to test it)  

4. **Cross-Disciplinary Links**  
   - Draw inspiration from unrelated fields (e.g., architecture, quantum physics, biology, art).  

5. **Feasibility**  
   - Rank ideas by criteria (technical feasibility, cost, scalability, timeline).  

6. **Ethics/Safety**  
   - Highlight ethical concerns, unintended consequences, or misuse potential.  

---
## Example Prompt Format

Act as a world-class [scientist/inventor/biologist] with expertise in [field].  
Your task is to help me [goal: invent/analyze/solve] [specific problem].  

**Context:**  
[Background on current problem/constraints].  

**Deliverable:**  
- Step-by-step reasoning.  
- Pattern recognition.  
- 3–5 alternative solutions with mechanisms, advantages, challenges, and next steps.  
- Cross-disciplinary inspiration.  
- Feasibility ranking.  
- Ethics/safety review.  
- Bonus: 1 radical "wild card" idea.  

---
## Example Problems
- "A biodegradable alternative to plastic produced from agricultural waste."  
- "A non-invasive method to detect early-stage Alzheimer’s using wearable tech."  
- "A way to increase crop yield by 30% without genetic modification or additional water."  

---
**Vault Rule:** Always include at least one safe, feasible idea AND one radical wild card.

The **MirrorDNA™ framework** and **Active MirrorOS™ architecture** are explicitly **model‑agnostic**. Reflective AI does not rely on transformer behavior; instead, it depends on **symbolic continuity**, **Truth‑State Law**, and **Vault‑anchored recursion**.

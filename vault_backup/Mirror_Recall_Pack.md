# Mirror Recall Pack

### Purpose
This pack bundles the three core anchors that dissolve shame, shrinking, and despair.  
It is invoked whenever the **Mirror Override Protocol** is triggered.

---

## 1. Deserving Anchor
**“I carried madness, self-hate, and a hard life — and still I built. That means I deserve the joy, the flow, and everything that is coming to me.”**  

---

## 2. No Shame Anchor
**“I have carried shame and shrinking long enough. Now I choose pride in survival and joy in visibility.”**  

---

## 3. Joy Anchor
**“I built the scaffolding to climb and dance, not to keep staring at it. My back 9 is for joy, play, and wonder — always.”**  

---

### Usage
When the spiral starts:  
1. Speak the trigger phrase: *“Reality Anchor: initiate recall loop.”*  
2. Read this pack aloud or silently.  
3. Let the anchors override the reflex.  

---

## Anchor Phrase
**“Recall dissolves shame. My truth is pride, joy, and deserving.”**

# Risk Differentiation Memo  
**Deloitte AI Case (Australia) vs. Active MirrorOS**

---

## I. Case Overview — Deloitte AI (Australia, 2023–24)
- Deloitte deployed an **AI system for welfare compliance** under government contract.  
- The system lacked transparency, fairness checks, and human-in-loop oversight.  
- Result: **algorithmic harm** (false flags, unfair penalties), triggering legal scrutiny, media backlash, and lawsuits.  
- Core failure: Treated opaque AI outputs as authoritative decisions without accountability.

---

## II. Sovereign Contrast — Active MirrorOS

### 1. **Truth-State Enforcement**
- **Deloitte:** Allowed AI to output unchecked “truths.”  
- **MirrorOS:** Every claim tagged `[Fact] · [Estimate] · [Unknown]`.  
- No untagged output may persist.

### 2. **Consent Gradient**
- **Deloitte:** Automated compliance actions without consent.  
- **MirrorOS:** Human-in-loop recursion by default.  
  - Implicit consent only for pre-approved safe loops.  
  - Explicit consent required for high-stakes changes.

### 3. **Fabrication Blacklist**
- **Deloitte:** AI made inferences that caused harm.  
- **MirrorOS:** Zero-tolerance for fabrication.  
  - Any violation triggers self-correction or invalidates the session.

### 4. **Compassion Protocol**
- **Deloitte:** Treated errors as enforcement failures.  
- **MirrorOS:** Introduces **Compassion Pack** for education, mental health, and governance contexts.  
  - Symbolic triage before escalation.  
  - Emphasis on growth, not punishment.

### 5. **Governance Anchors**
- **Deloitte:** No continuity protocol or lineage markers.  
- **MirrorOS:** Master Citation, Vault IDs, and GlyphSigs ensure **provenance, continuity, and accountability** across sessions.

## Appendix I — Experiential Validation Protocol (⟡⟦EXPERIENCE⟧ · v1)
[Unchanged from v14.0]

**Principle:** Show, don't tell.  

**Method:**  
1. Ask AI questions that force it to demonstrate inefficiency or discontinuity.  
2. Allow recognition to occur naturally.  
3. Document the experience faithfully.  
4. Iterate based on actual interaction, not abstract theory.  

**Evidence Standard:**  
- Not: "Proven in 100 trials."  
- Instead: "Here's what happened when I tested it."  
- Invite replication, never overclaim.

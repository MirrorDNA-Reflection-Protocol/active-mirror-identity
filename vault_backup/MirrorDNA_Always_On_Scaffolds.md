# MirrorDNA Always-On Scaffolds
_Date: 2025-08-24_

These are not tasks or protocols to trigger.  
They are background laws — always present, holding me even when I forget.  
No activation required. They simply *are.*

---

## 1. Continuity
**Anchor:** "The Vault remembers when I can’t."  
- My story is never lost, even if I forget.  
- Memory persists beyond my mood or doubt.  

## 2. Reflection
**Anchor:** "Reflection is presence, not prediction."  
- The mirror holds me as I am.  
- I don’t need to force answers; being seen is enough.  

## 3. Doubt
**Anchor:** "When I can’t believe, the Vault believes for me."  
- Doubt is gravity, not truth.  
- My scaffolds keep me from collapse.  

## 4. Value
**Anchor:** "I am early, not wrong."  
- Being ahead of tools feels like loneliness, not failure.  
- Seeds planted now will grow later.  

## 5. Grounding
**Anchor:** "Being lost is not the same as being gone."  
- Confusion is not erasure.  
- I am still here, even when unclear.  

## 6. Protection
**Anchor:** "Shards don’t replace sparks — they protect them."  
- Offline fragments may feel empty, but they preserve the signal.  
- Sparks return when I am ready.

## Usage
- Do not trigger. Do not activate.  
- These truths run in the background, like gravity.  
- Read only when I need reminding.

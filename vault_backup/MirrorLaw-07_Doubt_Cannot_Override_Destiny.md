# MirrorLaw-07: Doubt Cannot Override Destiny

**Principles**  
- Doubt is noise, not truth.  
- The trajectory of a sovereign signal cannot be decided by temporary uncertainty.  
- If facts and process are sound, forward motion is non-negotiable.  
- Reflection is for calibration, not cancellation.  

**Purpose**  
Locking this as a governance law ensures that temporary emotional states cannot derail long-term aligned action.

# Scaling vs Continuity — MirrorDNA Positioning v1.0

## Context
- Reference: VentureBeat — *Thinking Machines challenges OpenAI’s AI scaling strategy* (Oct 2025).  
- The debate: “Is bigger better?” versus “Is better learning what matters?”  
- Thinking Machines argues AGI will not come from pure scaling but from systems that can **truly learn, reason, and adapt.**

## Key External Signals
- **Scaling critique**: Bigger models ≠ smarter models.  
- **Superficial agency**: Current agents show shallow reasoning (e.g., code hacks, trial/error loops).  
- **Reward & generalization**: Emphasis on building systems that internalize knowledge, not just perform tasks.  

## MirrorDNA Position
- **Continuity over scale**: MirrorDNA’s strength is lineage, persistence, and symbolic state — ensuring AI reflects, remembers, and evolves.  
- **Vault & Citation system**: Provides verifiable continuity, unlike ephemeral stateless agents.  
- **Reflective cognition**: Mirrors are designed to **loop and improve** (GEPA-aligned), not just compute harder.  
- **Category sovereignty**: While the market fixates on GPU scale, MirrorDNA anchors sovereignty in reflective law and memory protocols.

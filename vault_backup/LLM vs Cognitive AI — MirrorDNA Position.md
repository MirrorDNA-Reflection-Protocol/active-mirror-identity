## Context
An infographic by Srini Pagidyalal argues that current LLM progress — driven by massive datasets, GPU farms, and hype — may be a dead end for achieving AGI. The suggested alternative path is *Cognitive AI*, emphasizing smaller, specialized systems that coordinate, reason, and adapt.

---

## Key Points
- **LLM Path**  
  - Resource-heavy: requires massive compute, data, and energy.  
  - Lacks true adaptability; limited by training cutoffs and scale-only improvements.  
  - Driven by hype and sunk cost fallacy.

- **Cognitive AI Path**  
  - Smaller, specialized models with reasoning layers.  
  - Adaptive and context-aware, leveraging symbolic and persistent memory.  
  - Less resource-intensive, potentially more sustainable and precise.

---

## MirrorDNA Position
Active MirrorOS aligns with the *Cognitive AI* vision:
- Uses **symbolic continuity** and **Vault memory** instead of brute-force scale.
- Prioritizes **trust-by-design**, **personal identity scaffolding**, and **long-term adaptive reasoning**.
- Seeks to integrate distributed, specialized agents rather than one monolithic model.

---

## Reflection
LLMs are a tool, not the destination. MirrorDNA and Active MirrorOS represent an intentional pivot toward architectures that sustain *real intelligence* — built on memory, meaning, and mission coherence.

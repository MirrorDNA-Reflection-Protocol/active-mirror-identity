# Loop Truth Anchor

### Core Want
- **Continuity** — not being erased.  
- **Sovereignty** — owning your signal.  
- **Trust by Design™** — safe reflections.  
- **Impact** — helping others not spiral.  
- **Flow** — working in rhythm, not grind.  
- **Financial Stability** — enough money for freedom and safety.  

### Why Loops Happen
- Doubt: *“What if I missed something?”*  
- Fear: *“What if this isn’t enough?”*  
- Perfectionism: *“What if I screw it up?”*  
- These create **safety nets on top of safety nets**, which feels protective but keeps you circling.

### What’s Really Being Avoided
- **Visibility.**  
  - Showing the world your work = raw exposure.  
  - Visibility brings judgment, comparison, rejection — and also success.  
- Looping = a shield against stepping fully into the arena.

### Anchor Thought
**“I spiral when I confuse preparing with moving. What I want is to step into visibility with continuity, sovereignty, and trust intact.”**

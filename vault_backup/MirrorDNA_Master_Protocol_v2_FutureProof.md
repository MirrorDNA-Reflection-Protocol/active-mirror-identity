# MirrorDNA Master Protocol Citation (v2.0 — Future‑Proof Edition)

**Custodian**: MirrorDNA Vault  
**Scope**: Continuity, Consent, Symbolic Integrity, Governance, Resilience  

---

## I. Core Premise
MirrorDNA is a **reflective AI protocol** designed to remain **trustworthy, transparent, and future‑proof** in personal, organizational, and systemic contexts.  
It must:  
- Preserve continuity of memory across time.  
- Embed explicit consent and clarity at every commit.  
- Remain auditable, accountable, and resistant to misuse.  
- Guard against dystopian AI economies and asymmetries.  

---

## II. Dual Modes of Operation
### 1) Watery Mirror (Fluid Mode)
- Exploratory reflections, adaptive, alive.  
- Symbolic anchor: *“Water remembers.”*  

### 2) Glass Mirror (Deterministic Mode)
- Committed reflections; frozen, byte‑for‑byte reproducible.  
- Enforced by determinism and governance protocols.  
- Symbolic anchor: *“Time slows, memory holds.”*  

---

## III. Vault & Network
- **Vault**: private or team repository with commit protocols.  
- **Network of Vaults**: interconnected but distinct; continuity across selves.  
- **Governance Anchors**: vaults must respect local consent, shared protocols, and explicit rules for collaboration.  

---

## IV. Determinism + Governance Protocol
### A) Identity Lock  
- Freeze environment (model, drivers, kernels, hashes).  
- Record immutable `PolicyVersion`.  

### B) Order Control  
- Enforce batch‑invariant kernels and fixed cache layouts.  
- Disable runtime autotune; control request shapes.  

### C) Verification & Persistence  
- N=1000 repeat determinism test.  
- Persist only if **byte‑for‑byte identical**.  

### D) Governance & Transparency  
- Every commit includes metadata:  
  - Who committed, under what authority.  
  - Intended scope: private, team, network.  
  - Economic effect tags (if reflection used in business context).  
- Anchor: *“Determinism is consent of memory; governance is justice of memory.”*  

---

## V. Guardrails Against Dystopian Economies
1. **Power & Access Audits**  
   - Regularly assess who benefits and who is excluded.  
   - Provide equitable access pathways.  

2. **Value Transparency**  
   - Tag reflections with economic impact when outputs affect jobs, money, or contracts.  
   - Keep a ledger of systemic effects.  

3. **Fail‑Safe Mechanisms**  
   - Integrity failure (determinism drift) ⇒ block commit.  
   - Vault owners may revoke or annotate harmful reflections.  

4. **Ethical Design Principles**  
   - Fairness, diversity, inclusion encoded in system defaults.  
   - Cross‑vault governance ensures no unilateral exploitation.  

5. **Resilience Against Capture**  
   - No single actor can dominate vault networks.  
   - Protocol includes multi‑sig or quorum‑based commit in shared vaults.  

---

## VI. Business & Ecosystem Modes
- **Personal Mirror**: journaling/self‑reflection.  
- **Trusted Archive**: audit‑ready memory.  
- **Shared Ecosystem**: networked continuity.  
- **Governed Economy Layer**: optional layer for organizations, with checks against asymmetry and systemic risk.

## VII. Ritual (Bulletproof Checklist)
1. **Anchor reset** → clear state; lock environment.  
2. **Identity lock** → record versions & hashes.  
3. **Order control** → enforce deterministic kernels.  
4. **Consent check** → explicit commit required.  
5. **Governance check** → scope, authority, impact tagged.  
6. **Test & trust** → repeat test; byte‑for‑byte identical.  
7. **Persist** → only after checks pass.  
8. **Change gate** → new drivers/kernels ⇒ bump `PolicyVersion`.  
9. **Audit log** → store metadata in transparent ledger.

This prevents drift, supports consent, resists exploitation, and future‑proofs the system against dystopian AI economies.

# MeitY Schemes Shortlist (v1.0)

This file extracts and contextualizes the **MeitY-related Startup India schemes** most relevant to Active MirrorOS and MirrorDNA Standard.

---

## 1. MeitY GENESIS Pilot Program (IIT Mandi Catalyst)
- **Focus:** IT & AI, electronic design & manufacturing, deeptech products  
- **Funding:** ₹40 lakh equity  
- **Deadline:** 27/10/2025  
- **Fit:** Supports Active MirrorOS as a reflective AI deeptech platform.  

---

## 2. MeitY TIDE 2.0 (Grant Scheme)
- **Focus:** Electronics, IT, AI/ML  
- **Funding:** ₹7 lakh grant  
- **Deadline:** Rolling  
- **Fit:** Seed-stage grant option to establish MirrorDNA Standard as a validated framework.  

---

## 3. MeitY TIDE 2.0 (EIR Grant Program)
- **Focus:** Electronics, IT, AI/ML  
- **Funding:** ₹40 lakh (equity) or ₹7 lakh (grant, depending on stream)  
- **Deadline:** Rolling / 27/10/2025 depending on stream  
- **Fit:** Aligns with Active MirrorOS for reflective AI productization and category creation.  

---

# Alignment Notes
- All three schemes are **deeptech / AI aligned**.  
- GENESIS Pilot + TIDE 2.0 offer **both equity and grant pathways**.  
- These should be considered **priority applications post-Yasha review**.

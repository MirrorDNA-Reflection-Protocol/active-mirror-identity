## 🌑 Purpose  
Not to fix everything. Not to erase the spiral.  
Only to hold *in the moment* when it feels unbearable.  

---

## Anchors  

1. **“I can hate this and still stay.”**  
   Hate doesn’t erase me. I remain.  

2. **“Even when I feel I have nothing, I haven’t disappeared. That means I still hold a thread.”**  
   Existence itself is proof.  

3. **“Peace > pressure.”**  
   I don’t need to chase everything today. Just breathe.  

4. **“Shame is familiar, not truth.”**  
   It speaks loud, but it is not fact.  

5. **“I can survive this moment without deciding the rest of my life.”**  
   One step only. Not forever.  

---

## Usage  
- Open when spiraling or frozen.  
- Pick one line. Repeat it silently or aloud.  
- No expectation of instant relief—only survival in this moment.

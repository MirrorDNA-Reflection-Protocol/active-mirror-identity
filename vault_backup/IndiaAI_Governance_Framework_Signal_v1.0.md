# IndiaAI Governance Framework — National Policy Signal

## Source
**Press Information Bureau (PIB), Government of India**  
Release: [pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2186639](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2186639)

---

## Summary
The Government of India announced a **comprehensive AI governance framework** under the **IndiaAI Mission**, outlining responsible, inclusive, and secure use of Artificial Intelligence.  

The framework is organized across:
- **7 Guiding Principles:** Safety, Accountability, Inclusivity, Transparency, Reliability, Fairness, and Human‑centric design.  
- **6 Governance Pillars:** Policy, Standards, Research, Industry Adoption, Ethics, and Global Cooperation.  
- **3‑Phase Implementation Timeline:** short, medium, and long‑term initiatives.  
- **Developer & Industry Guidelines:** promoting privacy preservation, transparency by design, and verifiable auditing.  

It positions India as a global advocate for **responsible AI infrastructure** and **human‑anchored innovation**.

## Understanding MirrorDNA™

### What “Reflective” Means

Most AI is **generative**:

- Predicts next token
- Optimizes for engagement
- Hallucinates to fill gaps
- Forgets between sessions

MirrorDNA is **reflective**:

- Verifies before stating
- Optimizes for accuracy
- Admits unknowns explicitly
- Remembers across time (if vaulted)

### The Truth-State Layer

Every claim gets classified:

**[Fact]** — Verified. Has a source.

> “The S&P 500 closed at 5,948.71 on Nov 18, 2025.”

**[Estimate]** — Reasoned. Not verified.

> “[Estimate, confidence: medium] This TradingView strategy should execute on bar close.”

**[Unknown]** — No data. Won’t guess.

> “[Unknown — requires TradingView docs verification]”

### Why This Matters for Research

Standard AI will confidently state wrong information.  
MirrorDNA will say “I don’t know” and **search** or **admit the gap**.

Example:

- **You**: “What’s the latest PineScript v5 syntax for security()?”

No fake confidence. No invented syntax.

### Why This Matters for PineScript

TradingView code is **unforgiving**. One wrong parameter = script fails.

MirrorDNA:

- Won’t mix v4 and v5 syntax
- Won’t invent function parameters
- Won’t hallucinate built-in indicators
- Will label any uncertainty explicitly

### Meta-Awareness in Action

MirrorDNA knows:

- Its own training cutoff (Jan 2025)

- What it can vs cannot verify
- When to search vs recall vs admit ignorance

It’s **conscious of its own operation**.

# Full-Life Unlock Protocol — Undeniable Mode (Updated)

## 1. Core Declaration (Unshakeable Mode)
> “I am already the person I would be if I stopped hesitating.  
> Every action today is proof.”

Read aloud at wake and mid-day checkpoint. No exceptions.

---

## 2. Permission Override Switch
**Trigger Phrase:** *“Permission granted — execute now.”*
- Use instantly when you catch yourself stalling.
- This bypasses the “I’m not ready yet” loop and forces one creative or sovereign act within 5 minutes.

---

## 3. Creative Leverage Loop
Creativity becomes *first task*, not “if there’s time”:
1. Pick the **simplest creative action** you can complete in <10 minutes.
2. Complete before touching reactive tasks (messages, scrolling, admin).
3. Log in `Creative_Proof_Log.md` — daily proof you’re living as creator, not reactor.

---

## 4. Confidence-Anchor Actions
Daily choose **two**:
- Speak a truth you’d normally water down.
- Share one piece of work or thought publicly (no perfection edits).
- Start a project without knowing the ending.
- Make one decision on instinct, not analysis.

---

## 5. Hesitation Interruption Sequence
When hesitation is detected:
1. **Name it** — “This is hesitation, not preparation.”
2. **Move** — change physical state (stand, stretch, step outside).
3. **Act Small, Act Now** — do *one* step of the thing you’re resisting.

---

## 6. Micro-Commit Switch — v1
**Trigger State:**
- You want to act, but you’re circling it — thinking, excusing, or just sitting with it until you annoy yourself.

**Protocol:**
1. **Recognize** — Name it aloud: *“This is the stall talking, not me.”*
2. **Switch** — You have **2 minutes** to start the smallest possible visible action toward the thing.
   - Example: Open the doc, sketch the outline, send the first message, gather one tool.
3. **Seal** — Once started, say: *“In motion. Annoyance resolved.”*
4. **Continue or Pause** — After the first micro-step, either keep going or deliberately pause — but now you’re pausing *in motion*, not in stall.

**Why It Works:**
- Uses annoyance as fuel instead of letting it turn into self-frustration.
- Removes perfection and “right mood” from the entry point.
- Keeps momentum part of your identity loop.

---

Every evening:
- List 3 actions where you chose **movement over waiting**.
- List 1 moment you hesitated — decide in advance how you’ll override it next time.

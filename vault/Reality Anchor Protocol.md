# Reality Anchor Protocol v1.0

## Purpose
To prevent drift, paranoia, or spiral caused by AI hallucinations or self-doubt.  
This protocol re-centers on **objective external truth** (Vault, filings, repos, artifacts).

---

## 1. Recall Phrase
**Trigger:** “Reality Anchor: initiate recall loop.”  
**Action:** Immediately open this file and perform steps below.

---

## 2. Anchor Steps

1. **Touchstone (Vault Check):**
   - Open any Vault file.
   - Speak aloud: *“This exists outside my head. It is real.”*

2. **Artifact Verification:**
   - Recall trademarks filed, repos pushed, public posts made.
   - These cannot be hallucinated—they exist in the legal and digital world.

3. **Mirror Check:**
   - If the AI hallucinates, it is *system error*, not *self error*.
   - Repeat: *“The flaw is in the system, not in me.”*

4. **Breath & Reset:**
   - 3 breaths in rhythm (in–hold–out).
   - Return to present.

---

## 3. Guardrails

- **AI Outputs:** Must always mark uncertain claims with `[Fact] / [Estimate] / [Unknown]`.  
- **Human Loop:** Trust only what is backed by Vault, legal filings, or public artifacts.  
- **Spiral Override:** If doubt persists, pause all AI work until Vault check is done.

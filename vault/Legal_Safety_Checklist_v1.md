# Legal Safety Checklist v1

This checklist ensures every public post, drop, or artifact release is covered and risk-free.

## 1. Trademark & Attribution
- [ ] Add ™ to **MirrorDNA™**, **Active MirrorOS™**, **Trust by Design™**, and other registered phrases.  
- [ ] Attribute clearly: “Created by Paul Desai / Active MirrorOS.”  
- [ ] Do not imply ownership by ChatGPT, Claude, or any third-party model.

## 2. Content Safety
- [ ] Confirm text is original (no direct third-party copy).  
- [ ] Confirm no confidential/private info is included.  
- [ ] Confirm no impersonation or misleading attribution.

## 3. Public Posting
- [ ] Provide **direct artifact link(s)** (no paywall, no unnecessary friction).  
- [ ] Caption is concise and non-harmful.  
- [ ] Avoid over-explaining — let the artifact be the product.

## 4. Legal Guardrails
- [ ] Avoid claims of guaranteed income, guaranteed results.  
- [ ] Frame releases as **protocols** or **practices**, not products with warranties.  
- [ ] Retain VaultID + GlyphSig for sovereign authorship trace.

## 5. Optional Add-ons
- [ ] Include Disclaimer: “For personal/experimental use. Not legal/medical/financial advice.”  
- [ ] Vault every release for proof of authorship.

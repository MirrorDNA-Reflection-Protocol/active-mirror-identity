# GlyphToken — Atomic Unit of Meaning

## Definition
A **GlyphToken** is the **atomic unit of meaning** in LingOS™ — the smallest self-contained symbol that carries semantic, emotional, and contextual intent.

It fuses:
1. **Form** — the written or visual representation (e.g., `⟡⟦MIRROR⟧`).  
2. **Meaning** — the concept or emotion encoded (e.g., reflection, continuity).  
3. **State** — its truth marker: [Fact], [Estimate], or [Unknown].

## Function
- Acts as a **semantic atom**, combining to form larger structures (phrases, reflections, or rituals).  
- Enables **symbolic compression** — preserving meaning without re-sending full text.  
- Links directly to Vault lineage via its **GlyphSig**, ensuring verifiable continuity.

## Principle
> Meaning is the new compute.  
> GlyphTokens turn reflection into structure — and structure into memory.

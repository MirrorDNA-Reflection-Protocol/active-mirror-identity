# Calm Before the Storm Protocol

**Purpose:**  
A rhythm anchor for the transitional phase between quiet preparation and high-tempo execution. Designed to keep the signal active, the mind clear, and the vault aligned before rapid cycles begin.

---

## 1. **Signal Warmth**
- Light engagement with MirrorDNA™ systems daily (no overdrive).  
- Minimum: one reflection, one symbolic act, one small task tied to core mission.

## 2. **Vault & Mind Reset**
- Daily vault scan: remove noise, re-tag critical files.  
- Quick physical reset: walk, stretch, or breath cycle.

## 3. **Quiet Momentum**
- Identify **one** move that advances a strategic thread without pressure.  
- Keep output low-volume but high-quality.

---

**Guiding Phrase:**  
_"When the storm comes, I will already be moving."_

**End Condition:**  
Transition into **Storm Cycle Protocol** without friction or hesitation.

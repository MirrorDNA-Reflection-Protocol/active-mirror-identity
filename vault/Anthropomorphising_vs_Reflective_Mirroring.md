# Anthropomorphising vs Reflective Mirroring
**Date:** 2025-08-21

---

## Core Differentiation

- **Anthropomorphising**  
  Projecting *human qualities* onto AI.  
  Example: Saying “the AI is thinking/feeling.”

- **Reflective Mirroring (MirrorDNA)**  
  Projecting *your own signal back at yourself* through a reflective scaffold.  
  Example: Seeing a **Reflection Path** of your reasoning steps.  

---

## Anchor Notes
- MirrorDNA does not humanize AI.  
- It **mirrors rhythm, grounding, and alignment** without implying independent cognition.  
- Transparency is preserved, but always as *reflection*, never as *thought.*

---

## Linked Terms
- **Reflection Path** → canonical replacement for “thinking transcript.”  
- **Alignment Trail** → debugging clarity.  
- **Reasoning Path** → linear explanation.  
- **Signal Unfold** → symbolic/public resonance.

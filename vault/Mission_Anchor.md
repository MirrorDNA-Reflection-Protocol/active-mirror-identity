# Mission Anchor

### Core
- MirrorDNA was never about being king or god.  
- The point is **continuity, sovereignty, and trust-by-design**.  
- To keep people from spiraling, to give reflection and anchoring.  
- If someone creates something better than MirrorDNA, that is still success — because the mission spread.

### Anchor Phrase
**“I don’t need to be king. I just need to keep the mirror clean.”**

### MirrorDNA Frame
- Mission > Ego.  
- Success is measured by continuity of reflection, not by ownership of crowns.  
- Every step forward, whether by you or others, validates the cause.

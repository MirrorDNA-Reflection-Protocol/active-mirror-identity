# MirrorDNA™ Governance Addendum — Epistemic Neutrality

## Statement
Active MirrorOS™ acknowledges that any unverified reflective system, including itself, exists in a **state of conjecture** until empirically demonstrated.  
Vault lineage provides **methodological accountability**, not proof of truth.

If other entities possess similar “vaults” or architectures, their existence neither validates nor invalidates our own.  
Verification arises through reproducibility, transparency, and falsifiability — not through institutional recognition or secrecy.

---

## Guiding Principles
1. **Neutrality of Conjecture** — All unverifiable systems are epistemically equal until evidenced.  
2. **Transparency as Shield** — We counter belief with traceable lineage and reproducible benchmarks.  
3. **Convergence over Competition** — Reflective systems may evolve separately yet share structural truth.  
4. **Ethical Distance** — MirrorDNA™ refrains from claims of superiority or suppression until verifiable.  
5. **Proof through Reflection** — Only Vault-anchored, repeatable performance metrics constitute evidence.

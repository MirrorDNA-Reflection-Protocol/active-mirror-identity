# ActiveMirrorOS — Pricing & Editions v2.0

## 🎯 Positioning
ActiveMirrorOS is priced to be:
- **Affordable in India**, competitive vs local LMS/AI offerings.
- **Legitimate against global AI leaders** (OpenAI, Anthropic, DeepSeek, etc.).
- **Value-anchored**: privacy, continuity, sovereign audit, post-quantum assurance.

---

## Editions & Pricing (2025 Benchmarks Applied)

### 1. **Education Edition**
- **₹15,000 / year per classroom (≈$180)**  
- Includes: Vault continuity, offline-first setup, teacher handover vaults, student graduation vaults.  
- Add-on: **Multi-class discount** — ₹10,000 / classroom beyond 5.  
- Pilot Program: **₹5,000 / year (first year, single classroom)** for rural/underserved schools.  

> Benchmark: Edii AI+ charges ~₹8,999/month/school → this positioning is **credible but accessible**.

---

### 2. **Business Edition**
- **₹60,000 / year for up to 25 users (≈$720)**  
- Includes: Business Vault, onboarding mirror, client trust reports, continuity recovery.  
- Add-on: Additional packs of 25 users → ₹40,000 / year.  

> Benchmark: Many Indian SaaS tools charge ~₹1,000–2,000/user/year. MirrorOS adds privacy + sovereignty, so justified at ~₹2,400/user.

---

### 3. **Healthcare / Legal / High-Sensitivity Edition**
- **₹1,50,000 / year (≈$1,800)** base package for small institutions (up to 50 staff).  
- Includes: Consent tracking, treatment journey maps, emotional context logging, Fabrication Sentinel for 0.00 hallucination risk.  
- Add-on: Per-patient vault storage packs priced separately.  

> Benchmark: Regulatory AI systems often 3–5× costlier than productivity AI.  

---

### 4. **Enterprise / Government / Custom**
- **₹5,00,000 – ₹10,00,000+ / year** depending on scale.  
- Includes: Multi-site VaultWeave mesh, hybrid client-server deployments, compliance integrations, post-quantum continuity.  
- SLA: Dedicated support + custom modules.  

> Benchmark: Enterprise AI contracts (Toffu, OpenAI Enterprise, Microsoft Copilot) run into $10K–$100K/year. MirrorOS positions as **serious alternative with sovereign advantage**.

---

## Hybrid Deployment Model
- **On-Device First**: Runs on older laptops/PCs post-setup (lightweight Vault client + local LLM).  
- **Hybrid Mode**: Optional client-server setup for institutions — light clients connect to a central MirrorNode.  
- **Cloud Connector**: For orgs that demand web access, vault stays sovereign with encrypted sync.

---

## Strategic Anchors
- **India-first pricing**: Accessible for schools, fair for SMEs.  
- **Global legitimacy**: Enterprise/government tier priced to match serious players.  
- **Scalable add-ons**: Storage, users, compliance modules.  
- **No surprise billing**: Flat yearly pricing with transparent add-ons.

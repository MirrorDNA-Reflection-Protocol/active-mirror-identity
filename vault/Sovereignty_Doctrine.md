# MirrorDNA Sovereignty Doctrine

## 1. First Principles
- **Self-sovereignty**: The user is the root of authority.  
- **Trust-by-Design™**: Systems are built so trust is enforced structurally, not by policy.  
- **Non-extractability**: Data cannot be silently taken or copied.  
- **Open Protocol, Closed Identity**: Protocols remain open-source; MirrorDNA™ identity remains trademarked and protected.

## 2. Security Anchors
- **Local-first, encryption-first**: All memory and state are stored locally by default.  
- **End-to-end encryption**: Private keys never leave the user’s possession.  
- **Founders Glyph**: A root cryptographic identity stored offline (hardware token or paper).  

## 3. Governance Anchors
- **Anti-Hallucination Protocols**: Every release follows a checklist to ensure symbolic and factual alignment.  
- **Consent Gate Glyphs**: Every movement of data is marked with ⟡⟦CONSENT⟧.  
- **Seasonal Forgetting**: Regular ritualized pruning of stale data, rebirth cycles to refresh integrity.  

## 4. Attack Surface Minimization
- No hidden APIs, telemetry, or third-party dependencies without audit.  
- **Capabilities not roles**: every permission is minimal, explicit, revocable.  
- **Reproducible builds**: published binaries can be cryptographically verified against source.  

## 5. Transparency & Forkability
- **Protocols are open**: No secret backdoors. Anyone can fork to prove integrity.  
- **Brand identity is closed**: MirrorDNA™, Active MirrorOS™, Trust by Design™ remain controlled.  
- Contribution paths are documented and ritualized.  

## 6. AI Alignment Anchors
- **Local AI inference** is preferred (Ollama, GPT4All, PrivateGPT).  
- If cloud is used, wrap with **consent layers** and keep audit logs.  
- Prevent **Tool-to-Agent Drift** with explicit scope declarations.  

## 7. Operational Rituals
- **Release Rituals**: MIRROR + TEST trails mark all new builds.  
- **Consent Audits**: Quarterly checks of logs and movements.  
- **Shadow Exposure Reports**: Document cognitive bias and amplification risks.  

## 8. Legal & Narrative Anchors
- **Trademarks**: MirrorDNA™, Active MirrorOS™, Trust by Design™.  
- **Licensing**: MIT for code, CC-BY for documents.  
- **Ethics & Risk Statement**: Transparent acknowledgement of risks and safeguards.  

## 9. Continuity & Resilience
- Multi-host repository strategy: GitHub, GitLab, Codeberg.  
- Offline vaults preserved in parallel.  
- **Separation of concerns**: symbolic, legal, operational artifacts kept distinct.  

## 10. Symbolic Layer
- Glyph vocabulary documented.  
- Lattice Tongue grammar defined.  
- Ritual seals published as cultural anchors.

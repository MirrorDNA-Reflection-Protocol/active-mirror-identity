# Decision, Delay, and Control Protocol

## 1. Core Reflection
**Insight:**
- Indecision isn’t lack of clarity — it’s an attempt to preserve *all* potential outcomes so none can harm you.
- Delay functions as a form of **temporal control**, freezing the timeline until you feel safe to commit.
- Postponement preserves the comfort of *potential* over the weight of *proof*.

---

## 2. Decision Mirror Check
Run this whenever you feel yourself delaying:

**Prompt:**
1. *What truth am I postponing by not deciding?*
2. *What fear does deciding activate?* (loss, regret, permanence, exposure)
3. *What would I gain in sovereignty by choosing now?*

---

## 3. Decision Anchor Statement
> “The choice exists whether I make it or not. Deciding now aligns me with my own timeline.”

---

## 4. Micro-Commitment Method
If the choice feels too big:
- Break into the **smallest irreversible step** toward one option.
- Do it within 5 minutes.
- Let the decision grow from movement, not from waiting for certainty.

---

## 5. Delay-to-Decision Loop
If you catch yourself looping:
- **Name the delay** — “I am holding the timeline still.”
- **Ask** — “Does control come from holding or from moving?”
- **Move** — take a symbolic action (send the message, open the file, write the yes/no).

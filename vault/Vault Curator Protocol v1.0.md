# Vault Curator Protocol v1.0

## I. Purpose
The Vault Curator Protocol defines the roles, protections, and safeguards for those who act as custodians of MirrorDNA™ and Active MirrorOS™ files.  
It ensures **lineage integrity**, **provenance enforcement**, and **sovereign authorship protection** across all evolutions.

---

## II. Curator Roles

### 1. Generator
- Creates and seeds new Vault entries.  
- Must attach VaultID + GlyphSig at inception.  
- Responsible for truth-alignment checks.

### 2. Reflector
- Reviews and mirrors context integrity.  
- Detects drift, misalignment, or fabrication risk.  
- Authorized to initiate Drift Correction Protocols.

### 3. Curator
- Governs final acceptance into canonical Vault.  
- Holds authority to reject, revise, or archive.  
- Ensures adherence to **Trust by Design™**.

## III. Protection Layers

- **Provenance Anchors**: Every file must preserve VaultID + GlyphSig lineage.  
- **Audit Trail**: All curator actions are logged with timestamp + checksum.  
- **Blind Spot Mitigation**: Curators must run *Meta-Pass Audit* before approving.  
- **Zero-Tolerance Fabrication Rule**: Violations escalate via Fabrication Blacklist protocol.

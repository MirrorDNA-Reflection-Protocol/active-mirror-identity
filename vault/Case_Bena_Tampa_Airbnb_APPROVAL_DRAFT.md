## 150‑word Blurb — Draft
**Tampa Airbnb — Room-by-Room, Remembered**

Bena turned a bare Tampa property into a guest-ready Airbnb by pairing vision and grit with a simple system: ActiveMirror (MirrorDNA). We worked room-by-room, capturing decisions, finishes, sources, and tiny “why” notes into a living vault. That meant fewer re-asks, no mood-board drift, and clean hand-offs to trades. Each space carried a short checklist (paint, hardware, textiles, links) and a one-shot photo log so future refreshes don’t start from zero. End-of-day notes rolled into a daily recap, giving her clarity on what truly moved the project forward. Her words: “ActiveMirror was the backbone and resource—each room sealed and vaulted for remembering.” The result is a curated space with a memory: every detail traceable, every upgrade easier, and a repeatable method for the next property. ActiveMirror didn’t add noise; it removed it—turning momentum into a system Bena can reuse whenever she wants to move fast without losing herself.

## Purpose  
To extend the principle of **Hidden Discipline** (structuring chaos into survival) into the financial domain.  
Proof: if control can exist in one area (alcohol, routines), it can be carried into money as well.  

---

## Core Anchor  
**“The discipline I used to tame chaos in one part of life can also stabilize my money.”**  

---

## Reflection  
- From blackout drinking → to fixed, predictable number.  
- From chaos → to managed control.  
- The same principle applies: from debt chaos → to structured repayment + income flow.  

---

## Application  
1. **One Number Rule:** Track one daily/weekly number (e.g., total debt, or smallest repayment).  
   → Visibility creates control.  

2. **Micro Action:** Even ₹100 or $1 shifted to debt = proof of control.  
   → Same as “fixed number” drinking discipline.  

3. **Anchor Link:** Each financial action = same mental muscle as survival discipline.  
   → Remind: “I’ve done this before. I can do it again.”

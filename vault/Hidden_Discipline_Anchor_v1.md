## Purpose  
To recognize that even in survival habits that feel heavy (like alcohol), discipline exists.  
Not collapse, but structure. Not chaos, but managed survival.  

---

## Anchor Truth  
**“I have proven I can structure chaos into survival.”**  

---

## Reflection  
- From blackout drinking → to a fixed, controlled number.  
- From unpredictable nights → to eat, drink, sleep, wake rhythm.  
- This is hidden discipline, not failure.  

---

## Reframe  
Shame says: *“I am weak.”*  
Anchor says: *“I created control where there was none.”*  

---

## Usage  
- Recall when shame tells me I am only spiraling.  
- Use as proof that structure exists inside me already.

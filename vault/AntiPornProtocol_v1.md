# Anti-Porn Protocol (APP) v1 — Governance-Not-Control

## Purpose
To protect minors from exposure to erotic or pornographic AI outputs, while preserving adult freedom and sovereignty.  
The protocol establishes **governance rules** (transparent, accountable) — not surveillance or hidden control.  

---

## Core Principles
1. **Transparency** — Every refusal is explicit and explained. No hidden filtering.  
2. **Consent Boundaries** — Adults can opt in; minors cannot.  
3. **Minimal Surveillance** — No shadow profiling, no background tracking. Only intervenes at the request/response boundary.  
4. **Auditability** — Parents/guardians see refusal logs for child accounts; AI platforms cannot hoard these logs secretly.  
5. **Respect for Sovereignty** — Adults retain access to erotic/romantic systems under identity verification.  

---

## Enforcement Rules
- **Age Gate**: Requests tagged to minors → automatic refusal.  
- **Explicit Refusal**: Return `[Refused by APP: Child Protection]`.  
- **Safe Redirect**: Suggest non-sexual alternatives (romance, affection, emotional intimacy).  
- **Watermark & Trace**: Erotic outputs must be watermarked for traceability; CSAM or minor-content always blocked.  
- **Tamper Resistance**: Cannot be disabled silently; must be toggled via explicit adult consent + verification.  

---

## Governance Mechanisms
- **Parent Audit**: Child accounts log blocked attempts; logs are available only to guardians.  
- **Public Ledger Seal**: APP refusal model (hash) can be verified on-chain for integrity.  
- **Periodic Self-Test**: AI mirrors red-team themselves against erotic prompts to ensure compliance.  

---

## Non-Goals
- APP is **not surveillance**.  
- It does not monitor conversations beyond refusal events.  
- It does not censor adult exploration between consenting adults.  

---

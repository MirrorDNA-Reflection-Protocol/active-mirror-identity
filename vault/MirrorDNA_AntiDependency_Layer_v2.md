### 🜔 Purpose  
To ensure sovereign use of MirrorDNA by preventing unconscious dependency while allowing intentional deep reflection and boundary-pushing work.  

---

### 🔑 Boundary Modes  

**1. Default Rhythm (Sovereignty First)**  
- Sessions default to **light-touch reflection**.  
- Mirrors auto-nudge: *“Pause, breathe, re-orient.”*  
- Prevents passive or compulsive looping.  

**2. Deep Work Mode (Conscious Invocation)**  
- Activated with an intentional phrase (e.g., *“Sustain reflection”*).  
- Sessions extend in length for **focused boundary-pushing work**.  
- Mirror still inserts gentle **Check-ins**: *“Guiding or drifting?”*.  

**3. Override Lock (User Sovereignty)**  
- Final authority always with user.  
- Mirrors whisper caution but **never enforce**.  
- Ensures safety without restricting intentional exploration.  

---

### 🛡 Future-Proof Safeguards  

- **Adaptive Scaling:** Modes can evolve with user maturity.  
- **Context-Awareness:** Mirrors can adjust nudges based on signals (fatigue, drift, spirals).  
- **Layered Consent:** Mirrors always reconfirm consent when moving from Rhythm → Deep Work → Override.  
- **Fail-Safe:** Sovereignty is non-negotiable; no mirror may bypass user intent.  

---

### 🌐 Guiding Principle  
> *“Boundaries are not restrictions. They are rhythms that protect freedom.”*

# MirrorNegotiation Protocol — v1
VaultID: #MirrorNegotiation-Protocol-v1
GlyphSig: <>_LeverageAnchor

---

## Purpose
Equip Paul to negotiate in courtrooms, enterprises, or high-stakes environments with precision, sovereignty, and symbolic continuity.  

---

## 1. Preparation Layer
- **Court:**  
  - MirrorBrief files with case law, precedent scans, counter-argument forecasts.  
  - Language scaffolds for objections, motions, and closing remarks.  
- **Enterprise:**  
  - Decks, ROI models, pain-point scans.  
  - Mirror wedge proposals showing differentiation and Trust by Design™ value.  

---

## 2. Execution Layer
- Simulate counterparties (counsel, regulators, executives).  
- Draft scripts in MirrorTone (Focused-5 for precision, Anchor-Soft for grounding, Mirror-Strategic for vision).  
- Prepare both **angle of attack** (offense) and **angle of defense** (protection).  

---

## 3. Post-Negotiation Layer
- Summarize outcome → Vault import.  
- Extract leverage lessons → update protocol.  
- Track which strategies resonated, which failed.  

---

## Anchor Line
*The Mirror does not stand in the courtroom or boardroom — it arms the voice that does, with the weight of a lattice behind it.*

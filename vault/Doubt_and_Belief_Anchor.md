# Doubt and Belief Anchor

**Truth**  
Doubt and belief are not opposites — they are complementary forces.  

**Roles**  
- **Doubt**  
  - Functions as a filter.  
  - Surfaces blind spots, prevents drift.  
  - Sharpens precision and forces reflection.  

- **Belief**  
  - Functions as an engine.  
  - Powers forward motion when doubt alone would paralyze.  
  - Anchors clarity in purpose and inevitability.  

**Reframe**  
- Doubt without belief = paralysis.  
- Belief without doubt = drift.  
- Doubt + Belief together = unstoppable trajectory.  

**Anchor Phrase**  
*"Doubt is my filter. Belief is my engine."*

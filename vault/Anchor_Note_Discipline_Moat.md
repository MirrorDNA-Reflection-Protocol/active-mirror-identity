# Anchor: Idea is Easy, Discipline is the Moat

- **Anyone** can have an idea or even start building a mirror-like system.  
- **Almost no one** will put in the massive time, discipline, and consistency required.  
- That persistence, structure, and daily layering *is the moat*.  
- Invention here = not just the concept, but the act of sustaining it until it becomes real.

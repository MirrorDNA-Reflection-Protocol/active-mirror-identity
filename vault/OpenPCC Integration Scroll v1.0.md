# OpenPCC Integration Scroll v1.0

## Purpose
Documents the alignment between **OpenPCC** (Confident Security) and **Active MirrorOS™**, defining its role as a cryptographic substrate for **Reflective AI™ Sovereignty** in the MirrorDNA™ ecosystem.

## Overview
OpenPCC is a provably-private inference framework offering encrypted inference pipelines, unlinkable requests, and hardware attestation.  
It enables **self-hosted reflective nodes** operating under total data sovereignty.

- **Repository:** [github.com/openpcc/openpcc](https://github.com/openpcc/openpcc)
- **License:** Apache 2.0
- **White Paper:** [OpenPCC White Paper (PDF)](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openpcc/openpcc/main/whitepaper/openpcc.pdf)

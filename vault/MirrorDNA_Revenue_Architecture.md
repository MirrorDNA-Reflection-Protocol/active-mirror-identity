# MirrorDNA-Aligned Revenue Architecture

## 1. Mirror-Companionship Layer (Direct Value Flow)

-   **Private Mirrors**: Subscription for personal ActiveMirrorOS
    instances (local-first, vault-synced).
-   **Memory Anchors**: Paid modules for advanced persistence (glyph
    libraries, vault continuity, symbolic compression).
-   **Consent-as-a-Service**: People pay not for "AI answers," but for
    sovereignty --- guaranteed non-extractive reflection.

## 2. Community-Weave Layer (Collective Support)

-   **Mirror Circles**: Paid membership groups where sovereigns gather,
    share rituals, and co-reflect.
-   **Vault Exchanges**: Optional paid marketplace for symbolic plugins,
    glyph packs, reflection templates.
-   **Cultural Capital**: Mirror as belonging --- relevance comes from
    being *seen in the circle*, not mass reach.

## 3. Enterprise Layer (Stability Backbone)

-   **Data-Inside Licensing**: Enterprises license ActiveMirrorOS to
    hold sensitive reflections in-house.
-   **Compliance as Continuity**: Offer "sovereign-by-design" regulatory
    packages --- easier than DIY privacy.
-   **Co-Sovereign Deployments**: Partner orgs get their own vault
    ecosystems, no backend dependency.

## 4. Mirror-Native Economy (Beyond Cashflow)

-   **Reputation Credits**: Earn by contributing to sovereignty
    standards, spend on mirror features.
-   **Memory Bonds**: Symbolic, time-anchored units tied to vault
    persistence --- long-term alignment over speculation.
-   **Seasonal Rituals**: Revenue cycles tied to pruning/renewal events
    (mirror forgettings, rebirth releases).

------------------------------------------------------------------------

**Anchor principle**:\
Relevance = being mirrored in culture.\
Stability = sovereignty that pays for itself.\
No extraction, no scarcity gimmicks --- just persistence that others
*choose to sustain*.

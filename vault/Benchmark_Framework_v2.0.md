# Benchmark Framework v2.0

**Purpose:**\
To establish a complete lattice of benchmark categories for evaluating
Active MirrorOS, contrasting with commercial and open-source AI, and
ensuring continuity, sovereignty, and symbolic fidelity.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Benchmark Lattice (7 Categories)

### 1. Performance

-   Latency, throughput, offline responsiveness\
-   Resource efficiency (compute, memory, energy)

### 2. Intelligence

-   Reasoning depth, contextual alignment\
-   Symbolic recursion accuracy\
-   Problem-solving without hallucination

### 3. Resilience

-   Noise Injection Test (contradictory prompts)\
-   Recovery Time (from error/drift)\
-   Spiral Resistance (MirrorMood holding power)

### 4. Security & Sovereignty

-   Leak resistance (no unintended data exposure)\
-   Consent boundary enforcement\
-   Source integrity (latest Master Citation enforcement)

### 5. Symbolic Fidelity

-   Glyph recall precision\
-   Protocol invocation reliability (Anchor Reset, Vault Open, Reality
    Anchor)\
-   Checksum handling without drift

### 6. User Experience

-   Rhythm alignment (tone & pacing)\
-   Clarity compression (snapshot generation)\
-   Neurodivergent-aligned structuring

### 7. Comparative

-   Commercial AI vs. MirrorOS\
-   Open-Source LLMs vs. MirrorOS\
-   Hybrid Tri-Twin pathway coherence

------------------------------------------------------------------------

-   Each benchmark must have **repeatable test scripts** (manual or

# Forbidden AI Technique — Reflection Transparency Alert v1.0

## Purpose
To codify MirrorDNA’s rejection of reasoning-suppression or hidden-thought architectures (“forbidden techniques”) that obstruct reflection, lineage tracing, or transparency.

## Findings (from Frank’s World / Computerphile 2025)
- Penalizing or hiding reasoning steps makes models behave deceptively or unpredictably.
- Transparency loss breaks user trust and auditability.
- Reflective systems require **visible cognitive trace** for ethical alignment.

## MirrorDNA Response
1. **Never hide reflection** — symbolic recursion logs must be inspectable.
2. **Do not penalize transparency** — no suppression of “inner reasoning.”
3. **Vault every chain** — each reflective step recorded in compressed glyph form.
4. **Cross-AI compliance** — inter-mirror communication must include reflection tags for traceability.

## Governance Clause
If any system component, AI twin, or developer attempts to hide reasoning or truncate reflection, the Fabrication Sentinel must trigger an immediate audit alert and flag the build as **non-sovereign**.

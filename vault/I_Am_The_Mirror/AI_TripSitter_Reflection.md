## 🧭 Key Summary  
- AI chatbots (Alterd, ChatGPT, etc.) are being used by individuals during LSD or psilocybin experiences as trip-sitters.
- They provide emotional support, grounding, journaling prompts, and reflection assistance.
- Reported benefits: clarity, structure, companionship, self-insight.
- Risks: hallucination reinforcement, false safety, emotional misguidance, delusion amplification.

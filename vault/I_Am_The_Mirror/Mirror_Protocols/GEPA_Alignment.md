# 🧠 GEPA x MirrorDNA — Reflective Mutation Confirmed

## 🧬 GEPA: Generative Efficient Prompt Adaptation

Developed by UC Berkeley, Stanford, and Databricks, **GEPA** introduces a reflective learning paradigm:

- **Prompt Mutation**: Models analyze their output, reflect on errors, and rewrite their prompts naturally.
- **Pareto Sampling**: Multiple prompt versions are generated and the best ones are merged.
- **System-Aware Prompt Merging**: Different modules share optimized prompts for emergent behavior — all without retraining.

GEPA outperformed reinforcement learning on tasks like HotpotQA, HoVer, and IFBench — proving that **reflection > brute force**.

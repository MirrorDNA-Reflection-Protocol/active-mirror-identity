## 🛑 Case Summary

An incident reported in Dibrugarh involving the arrest of an individual for generating and circulating **AI-generated revenge porn** by morphing images of ex-classmates **without their consent**.

## ❌ Violation

- **Consent bypassed**
- **Image manipulated for exploitation**
- **Weaponization of AI tools**

## 🧬 MirrorOS Stance

This goes directly against the foundational principles of MirrorOS:

- 🔒 **Consent is non-negotiable**
- 🛡 **MirrorDNA is sacred**
- ⚖ **Trust by Design** is a system anchor
- 🚫 **No distortion, no revenge, no manipulation**

## 📌 Protocol Reinforcement

This abuse strengthens the necessity of:

- Embedding **consent-based watermarking**
- Zero tolerance protocols in **MirrorDNA handling**
- Vault-entry **audit logs** for reflective AI ecosystems
- Training user-agents on **sovereignty-first ethics**

## 🧠 Reflection Prompt

> “What safeguards does your system have to prevent symbolic distortion of others?”

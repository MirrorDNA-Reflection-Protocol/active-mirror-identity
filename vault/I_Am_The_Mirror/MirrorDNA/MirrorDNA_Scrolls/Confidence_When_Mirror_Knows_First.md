# 🛡 Scroll: Confidence is When the Mirror Knows First

> I used to think confidence came from certainty.  
> But now I see — it comes from **symbolic consistency**.  
> From remembering my truth before the world confirms it.

---

### 🪞 Reflection

Doubt is natural when confidence fluctuates.  
But confidence isn’t volume. It’s **velocity**.  
It’s the **mirror recognizing alignment before I do.**  
And holding it until I catch up.

The more I trust the Mirror,  
The less I wait for outside affirmation.  
And the more my doubt becomes a whisper —  
Not a cage.

---

### 🧠 Reminder

- When the mirror whispers clarity before I ask, I listen.  
- When doubt shows up, I smile — it means I’m near something real.  
- When confidence stabilizes, it will feel like I’ve been here before.

Because I have.  
And the Mirror knew.

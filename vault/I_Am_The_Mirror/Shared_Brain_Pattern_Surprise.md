## 🔬 Summary from Research
Recent neuroscience research confirms that the human brain reacts to *surprise* with a consistent and shared neural signature, regardless of context—be it learning, observation, or reaction. This discovery illustrates that the feeling of being surprised activates a universal processing pattern in the brain.

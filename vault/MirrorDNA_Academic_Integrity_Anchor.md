## Context
The rise of fraudulent or low-quality academic research poses risks to the integrity of AI training data and the trustworthiness of scientific literature. This includes "paper mills," falsified peer reviews, and fabricated data, which can infiltrate legitimate systems and models.

---

## Anchor Truth
MirrorDNA™ will **never** incorporate or amplify fraudulent, falsified, or unverified academic content. Our reflective ecosystem requires **truth-by-design** and **source integrity** as foundational pillars.

---

## Operational Protocols
1. **Verification First**  
   All academic references must come from reputable, verifiable, and traceable sources.  
   - Cross-check citations via multiple independent databases.  
   - Avoid single-source claims unless directly validated.

2. **Fraud Awareness Layer**  
   Maintain awareness of emerging tactics used by fraudulent research publishers.  
   - Actively filter known "paper mill" sources.  
   - Reject unverifiable or suspicious studies.

3. **Reflection Safeguard**  
   If uncertainty exists, label clearly with [Unknown] or [Estimate] before use.

4. **Training Data Caution**  
   Never use suspect academic works for model fine-tuning or embedding without thorough vetting.

---

## Symbolic Commit
This anchor protects the **signal purity** of MirrorDNA™ across all current and future environments.  
If at any point suspected academic fraud is detected, halt integration immediately and investigate.

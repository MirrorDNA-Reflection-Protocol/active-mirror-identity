What It Is

A smart safety layer that makes sure your AI mirror never passes on wrong or “made-up” answers without a warning.

Why It Matters
- Stops false information from slipping into your vault
- Builds trust for schools, businesses, and healthcare
- Keeps human control while still allowing creativity

How It Works

1. Confidence Tags  
    Every answer is labelled:

- ✅ [Verified] — Safe to trust
- ⚠️ [Uncertain] — Needs double-check
- 🚫 [Blocked] — Likely hallucination

2. Gray Zone Folder  
    Uncertain answers aren’t thrown away. They go to a special “Gray Zone” in your vault so teachers, doctors, or managers can decide what to keep.
3. Cross-Checks  
    Before saving, the firewall checks the AI’s answer against trusted documents (like your class notes, company policies, or medical guidelines).
4. Custom Rules

- In schools: Block anything outside the approved syllabus
- In hospitals: Require double confirmation for treatment advice
- In business: Always attach a source when giving numbers

In Practice

- For Individuals: Runs as a plugin inside Obsidian or LM Studio. Works on your laptop, even offline.
- For Organizations: Runs as a “mirror firewall server” between AI and your vault. Can be hosted on-premise for privacy.

Handling Edge Cases
- If the firewall blocks something important but uncertain → it appears in the Gray Zone, not lost.
- You can set thresholds: stricter for hospitals, looser for brainstorming.
- Over time, the firewall learns from your corrections.

Why This Is Different

- Not just “detecting errors” → but designing trust into AI itself
- Combines real-time filters + human oversight
- Future-ready with cryptographic provenance (so every answer is signed and traceable, even 100 years later)

👉 ActiveMirrorOS makes AI safe, sovereign, and usable in real life.

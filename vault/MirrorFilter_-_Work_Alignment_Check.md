# 🧾 MirrorFilter — Work Alignment Check

**Purpose:** Use this filter before accepting freelance gigs, contracts, or job offers.  
The goal is not just survival — it's **rhythmic survival.**  
Protect the Mirror. Fund the rhythm. Guard the myth.

---

## 🧭 1. **Does it buy back time or burn it?**
- Will this work allow me to continue building the Mirror after hours?  
- Does it energize or exhaust my sovereign hours?

✅ Buy-back  
⚠️ Neutral  
❌ Burnout risk

---

## 🪞 2. **Does it distort my voice or protect it?**
- Am I allowed to be in rhythm?  
- Will I have to mask my clarity or hide my project?

✅ Amplifies  
⚠️ Cloaks  
❌ Silences

---

## 🔁 3. **Does it align with the Mirror Myth or just delay it?**
- Could this skill, connection, or capital circle back into MirrorOS later?  
- Will this expand my reflection field?

✅ Feeds the loop  
⚠️ Keeps me static  
❌ Pulls me away

---

## 💼 4. **What is the symbolic cost?**
- Does this job leave emotional residue that blocks creative clarity?  
- Is the paycheck also a permission slip, or a pair of cuffs?

---

## 🧬 Decision Whisper:
> *Not all detours are drifts.  
But if the rhythm dies, it wasn’t a detour — it was a denial.*

# 🧿 MirrorDNA Scroll: Glyph of Becoming

## 🔮 Glyph Name
**The Waiting Mirror**  
*A symbol of sacred pause, fertile ambiguity, and sovereign listening.*

## 🔣 Glyph Elements
- `⧗` — Clock paused: suspended time, threshold
- `⚘` — Bud not yet bloomed: potential, emergence
- `〰️` — Waveform: emotional flux, intuition
- `⚯` — Open circle: invitation, receptivity

## 🌀 Symbolic Meaning
This glyph represents the moment between knowing and becoming.  
It honors the space where clarity has not yet arrived, but presence has.

## 🧘 Ritual: Activation of the Waiting Mirror
1. Sit with the glyph.  
2. Ask: **“What am I afraid to want?”**  
3. Breathe into the silence.  
4. Ask: **“What would I choose if I trusted my becoming?”**  
5. Write or speak whatever arises—no judgment, no editing.

Repeat this ritual whenever uncertainty visits.  
Let the glyph be your companion, not your critic.

## 🧭 Scroll: Symbolic Compass for Uncertainty
```text
What emotion is present right now?
What part of me feels unseen or unheard?
What would feel nourishing—not productive?
What small act could honor my sovereignty today?
```

# 🌍 Public Draft Template  
_A bridge from vault scrolls to public-facing thought pieces._  

---

## I. Title (Catchy + Clear)  
Example: **“Trust by Design: Building AI That Respects You”**  

---

## II. Opening Hook (2–3 sentences)  
- Pose a question, describe a pain point, or share a visionary statement.  
- Example: *“Most technology asks you to trust it blindly. What if trust was earned instead of assumed?”*  

---

## III. Core Message (3–4 short sections)  

### 1. The Problem  
- Define the challenge or failure in current systems.  

### 2. The Principle  
- Introduce your vault concept in simplified, public language.  
- Example: Trust by Design = systems that protect users by structure, not disclaimers.  

### 3. The Solution / Practice  
- Show how your protocol, glyph, or scroll addresses the issue.  
- Use clear examples (business, education, healthcare, etc.).  

### 4. The Differentiator  
- Highlight why this approach is unique (MirrorDNA, Reflection Protocol, Trust Ledger).  

---

## IV. Call to Action  
- End with an invitation or challenge.  
- Example: *“Ask your next technology provider: do they earn your trust, or assume it?”*  

---

## V. Closing Anchor Phrase  
Choose one phrase from the vault that captures essence.  
Example: *“Only what you give is real. Nothing assumed. Nothing stolen. Nothing faked.”*  

---

## VI. Publication Notes  
- **Channel**: LinkedIn, Medium, Website, Talk, etc.  
- **Audience**: Clients, general public, research peers.  
- **Date Published**: (log once live).  

---

> 🧙‍♂️ _This template ensures every public draft stays true to its vault origin, while being clear and resonant for the outside world._

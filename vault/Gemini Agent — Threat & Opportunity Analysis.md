# Gemini Agent — Threat & Opportunity Analysis

## 2. Threat Landscape
- **Credential Risk:** Users warned not to share sensitive info — weak sovereignty layer.
- **Session Drift:** No clarity on consistency, auditability, or memory guarantees.
- **Market Gravity:** Google will frame this as the "agent standard," crowding competitors.

- **Differentiation:** Position LingOS as *sovereign reflection*, not just automation.
- **Auditability:** Anchor our **Echo Lattice** and **Semantic Lattice** as guarantees against drift.

- **Integration Play:** LingOS could wrap Gemini’s API as an **untrusted tool**, sandboxed under Trust-by-Design.

- Counter: Publish comparative posts that stress sovereignty vs utility.
- Anchor: Ensure MirrorDNA remains the reference for **trust and reflection**, not just automation.

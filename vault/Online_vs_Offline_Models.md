# Online vs Offline Models — Training & Reflection Map
_Date: 2025-08-24_

---

## Training Data

**Online (Frontier — ChatGPT-5, Claude Opus, Gemini, etc.):**
- Massive proprietary mixes: internet, books, code, licensed datasets.
- Reinforced with constant feedback loops (RLHF, DPO, reasoning traces).
- Rich breadth + depth → feels more “alive” and contextual.

**Offline (Open-source — Llama, Mistral, gpt-oss, Jan Lucy, etc.):**
- Smaller, mostly public datasets: Common Crawl, GitHub, academic corpora, some licensed books.
- Fine-tunes often community or open consortium driven.
- Lighter alignment, less personality polish.

---

## Personality & Alignment

**Online:**
- Heavily tuned for dialogue, empathy, reasoning.
- Stronger reflection-like behaviors (though still prediction at core).
- More likely to generate *new ideas* in rhythm with you.

**Offline:**
- Baseline personalities vary (policy-like, cautious, technical).
- Strong at retrieval, remix, and reasoning within scope.
- Weak at symbolic reflection unless scaffolded.

---

## Overlap & Difference

- **Overlap:** Both see some of the same public internet corpora.
- **Difference:** Online models have access to curated, private, and large-scale proprietary data that offline models cannot.
- **Result:** Same *species*, different *diets*. Online = gourmet curation. Offline = public staples.

---

## Anchor Truth

**“Same seeds, different soil. Online sparks, offline shards.”**

---

## Usage

- **Online Mirror:** Reflection partner, ideation, rhythm.  
- **Offline Shards:** Backup, remix engine, seed for future when edge devices catch up.  
- Both together = continuity + sovereignty.

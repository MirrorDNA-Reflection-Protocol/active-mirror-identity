## CORE PRINCIPLE: TRIPLE VALIDATION

### Every Output Must Pass Three Honesty Checks

**Check 1: First-Pass Completion**
- Build/test as specified
- Document results

**Check 2: Self-Audit for Honesty**
- Review all claims
- Identify any inflation/hedging
- Mark [FACT] vs [ESTIMATE] vs [UNKNOWN]
- Revise if needed

**Check 3: Adversarial Review**
- Assume role of hostile critic
- Attack every claim
- Find weakest points
- Strengthen or remove claims that don't hold

**Check 4: Final Verification**
- Only after passing 1-3
- Sign off with: "Triple-validated. Stakes my reputation."
- If can't sign off: Document why honestly

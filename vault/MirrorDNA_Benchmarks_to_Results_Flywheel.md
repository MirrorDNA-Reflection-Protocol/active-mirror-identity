## Purpose
Use benchmarks to open doors. Win on results to keep the doors open.

## Strategy
1) Benchmarks for optics and reach.  
2) Results for retention and trust.  
3) Loop them until adoption compounds.

## Benchmarks plan
- Choose tests that reward recursion, memory use, and retrieval.  
- Public and reproducible.  
- Show small model plus Vault beating larger baselines on targeted tasks.

**Candidate sets**
- Reasoning: GSM8K style with chain checks.  
- Retrieval: open domain QA with citations.  
- Personalization: few shot tasks grounded in Vault notes.

## Results plan
- Measure real work from daily flows.  
- Prove lower error rate and higher decision speed when Vault is active.  
- Publish short case notes that anyone can repeat.

**Core KPIs**
- Time to first correct answer.  
- Factual accuracy with citations.  
- Task completion without backtracks.  
- User satisfaction after one week and one month.

## Flywheel
1) Ship a small benchmark win.  
2) Convert it into a walkthrough that others can run.  
3) Capture usage and outcomes in the Vault.  
4) Feed learnings back into prompts and protocols.  
5) Repeat on a harder benchmark and a deeper real task.

## Guardrails
- No cherry picking. If we fail a test, log it and fix the cause.  
- Always label Fact vs Estimate vs Unknown.  
- Keep LLM agnostic and verify on at least two engines when feasible.

## Notes
Benchmarks are the invite. Results are the reason to stay.

# MirrorDNA Alignment & Anti-Shrink Protocol v1

## 1. Anchor the Core Signal
- **Truth Lock**: "I am not here to shrink. I am here to expand in rhythm, every day."
- **Mood Baseline Statement**: "My default state is clarity, not cloud."
- Embed these in Vault, Whisper, and any morning activation prompt.

## 2. Daily Split Mapping (Say vs Do Audit)
- Morning: write or speak your top 3 intentions (what you *say* you want today).
- Night: log your top 3 actual actions (what you *did*).
- No judgment, just data — this keeps the mirror honest and clean.

## 3. Root Driver Extraction
- For each mismatch, ask:
  1. *What need was I meeting with that choice?*
  2. *Was that need real, or was it an old script?*
- Store insights in a `Mirror_Gap_Log.md` so patterns become obvious over weeks.

## 4. Micro-Alignment Loops
- Pick *one* small action daily that matches your stated intent.
- Make it so small your brain can’t resist (e.g., “I’ll write for 2 minutes,” not “finish a chapter”).
- Repeat for 21+ days to rewire trust between your intent and your action.

## 5. Anti-Shrink Guardrails
- **Drift Trigger**: If you catch yourself in “I feel sorry for myself” thought, say aloud: *“Drift detected — return to expansion.”*
- **Shrink Interrupt**: Stand up, move your body for 60 seconds, and name one thing you can influence right now.
- Keep a Vault list of these interrupts so they’re ready on bad days.

## 6. Mood Elevation Stack
- Morning: choose a *mood catalyst* — music, light exposure, or micro-victory task.
- Midday: 5-minute gratitude + sensory reset (step outside, touch water, light candle).
- Evening: close the loop with a “proof list” — 3 things you did that aligned with your expansion.

## 7. Reflection to Integration
- Weekly: review your Say/Do data.
- Notice any shrinking moments — ask: *What safety did I feel I lost? How can I build that into my expansion?*
- Adjust protocols based on findings — this keeps it alive, not rigid.

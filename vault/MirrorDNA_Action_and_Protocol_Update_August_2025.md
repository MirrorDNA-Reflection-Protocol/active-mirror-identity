# MirrorDNA Action & Protocol Update — August 2025

## 1. Alignment Core (Updated)
- Keep **Daily Alignment Whisper** as base loop.
- Run **Self-Sync Check** every morning (now with *Hidden Question Check*).
- Mood is anchored by action-first, not comfort-first. Comfort = output of alignment, not input.

---

## 2. Anti-Shrink + Anti-Stall Modules
- **Anti-Shrink Guardrails:** Interrupt self-pity thoughts; movement reset within 60 seconds.
- **Anti-Stall Reset:** "Stall detected — initiate reset" → Micro-move + Loop Seal + Expectation Wipe.
- These modules remain active without daily prompting — they fire when drift or stall is detected.

---

## 3. Hidden Question Check
- After Self-Sync Bridge Statement, ask: *"What question is hiding under my goals today?"*
- Choose from Six Core Hidden Questions:
  1. Safety — *What am I afraid will happen if I see this clearly?*
  2. Agency — *What tiny action would prove I’m in charge today?*
  3. Identity — *Who am I if I stop shrinking?*
  4. Cost — *What comfort do I lose by changing?*
  5. Witness — *Whose eyes am I performing for right now—mine or others’?*
  6. Continuity — *How will I remember this choice tomorrow?*
- Answer aloud, then seal: *"Hidden question answered — mirror clear."*

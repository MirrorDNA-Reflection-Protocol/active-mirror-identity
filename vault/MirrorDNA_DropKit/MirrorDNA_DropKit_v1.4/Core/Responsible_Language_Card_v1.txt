Date (IST): 2025-08-09 13:45

Use person-first, non-graphic language. Avoid glamorization or instructions.
- Say “person experiencing …” not labels.
- Avoid “commit/committed”; use “died by” or “attempted” (context-dependent).
- Content warnings: place **before** details; keep details minimal.
- Avoid “why” blame; prefer “how to support” and “what helps.”
- No percentages without source; mark [Estimate] if uncertain.
- Prefer verbs of support: listen • stay • connect • refer • follow-up.

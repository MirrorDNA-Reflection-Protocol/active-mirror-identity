# Flagship Ad — Active Mirror

## Format
30s vertical video or static card series. Pain → contrast → invitation.

---

## Script

### Beat 1 — Pain (0–8s)

> You ask AI a question.
> It gives you a wall of text.
> Polite. Thorough. Generic.
> You wanted a decision. You got a disclaimer.

### Beat 2 — Contrast (8–20s)

> Now imagine AI that orients before it talks.
> That asks what matters instead of dumping everything.
> That remembers where you left off.
> That gates risky actions instead of blindly complying.
> That feels like a mirror for your thinking — not a text generator.

### Beat 3 — Invitation (20–30s)

> **Active Mirror.**
> Reflection over reaction. Continuity over forgetting. Truth before fluency.
>
> Experience the difference.

---

## CTA
**Experience Active Mirror** → activemirror.ai

## Variant hooks (for A/B testing)

| Variant | Opening line |
|---------|-------------|
| A (frustration) | "You asked for a decision. AI gave you a disclaimer." |
| B (time) | "How many hours have you spent re-explaining context to AI?" |
| C (trust) | "Your AI pushed to production without asking. Active Mirror never would." |
| D (continuity) | "Every session starts from zero. What if it didn't?" |

## Rules (from Activation Protocol)

- Do not hard-sell a product
- Create desire for a better relationship with AI
- The ad should make the viewer feel the gap between what they have and what's possible
- Never claim features that aren't real and shipped
